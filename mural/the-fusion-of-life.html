The Fusion of Life

🇦🇷 San Juan, Argentina, Maanso Meeting II · 2018 · 9m x 2.5m

Mural EventMix Media

Nine metres wide by 2.5 metres high, painted in mixed media for the second Maanso Meeting in San Juan, Argentina, in 2018. The mural represents the fusion of heart and brain, love and reason working towards a single purpose: fifty per cent love, fifty per cent reason, one hundred per cent dedication. It is one of the largest walls in the portfolio and was painted as part of a group mural event.
