California Bear

🇦🇷 Virreyes, Buenos Aires, Argentina · 2025 · 3m x 2.5m

Street ArtSpray Paint

A friendly bear in green sunglasses under a crescent moon, sprayed three metres wide by 2.5 metres high in Virreyes, San Fernando, in the north of Greater Buenos Aires, Argentina. The mural sits on a street wall in the neighbourhood and is painted in a flat, cartoon-leaning style, with the moon doubling as the light source for the whole scene.
