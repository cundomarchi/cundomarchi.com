BULLL$HIT

🇮🇹 Turin, Italy · 2025 · 5m x 2.2m

Street ArtSpray Paint

A bull charging out of a wall in electric magenta and teal, five metres wide and 2.2 metres high, painted with spray paint in Turin, in the Piedmont region of northern Italy. The piece was made as a collaboration with the Italian artist Ades, whose lettering runs alongside the animal. The bull is built almost entirely from complementary colours, with no black outline holding the shape together.
