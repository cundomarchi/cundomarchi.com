Inner Child

🇦🇷 San Fernando, Buenos Aires, Argentina · 2025 · 2.5m x 2m

Street ArtSpray Paint

A child with his eyes closed and his hands together, a light breaking open between his palms, sprayed 2.5 metres wide by two metres high across the shutters of a shopfront in San Fernando, Buenos Aires, Argentina. Rays of red and blue radiate from the figure to the edges of the metal, and lines of yellow light run out from his hands along the bottom of the wall. Like the other shutter murals, it is only fully visible when the shop is closed.
