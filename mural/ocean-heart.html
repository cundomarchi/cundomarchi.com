Ocean Heart

🇲🇽 Bonfil Urban Mural Fest, Acapulco, Mexico · 2022 · 7m x 2.5m

Mural EventSpray Paint

A heart made of coral resting on the ocean floor, seven metres wide by 2.5 metres high, sprayed for the Bonfil Urban Mural Fest in Acapulco, Guerrero, Mexico. The image is a reminder that reefs are the actual heart of the sea, painted in a coastal city where the health of the water is part of daily life. Bonfil is a neighbourhood mural festival that brings artists to paint the walls of the community.
