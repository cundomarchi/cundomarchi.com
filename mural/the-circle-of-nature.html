The Circle of Nature

🇦🇷 Buenos Aires, Argentina · 2022 · 2.2m x 3m

Commission WorkExterior Paint & Brush

A parrot at rest inside a golden spiral, framed by moonlight and jungle leaves, 2.2 metres wide by three metres high. Painted with exterior paint and brush as a private commission in Buenos Aires, Argentina. The composition is built on the golden spiral, so the bird's body, the leaves and the moon all sit on the same curve.
