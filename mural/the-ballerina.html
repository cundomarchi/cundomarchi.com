The Ballerina

🇦🇷 San Fernando, Buenos Aires, Argentina · 2025 · 2.5m x 1.2m

Commission WorkExterior Paint & Brush

A ballerina caught mid turn, her tutu open like a flower, painted in soft pastels with exterior paint and brush on a shopfront wall in San Fernando, Buenos Aires, Argentina. The mural is 2.5 metres wide by 1.2 metres high and was made as a commission for the business, so the figure is sized and placed to work with the shop's own frontage.
