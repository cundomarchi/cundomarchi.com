The Viking

🇸🇪 Malmö, Sweden · 2025 · size TBC

Street ArtSpray Paint

A Swedish viking raising a spray can in one hand and a glass in the other, painted as a collaboration on a wall in Malmö, in the south of Sweden. The figure swaps the usual axe and horn for the tools of the wall itself, which makes it a portrait of the painting scene in the city as much as of a viking.
