You Are What You See

🇨🇭 Sierre, Switzerland · 2025 · 3.5m x 2.2m

Street ArtSpray Paint

A single radiant eye, 3.5 by 2.2 metres, sprayed on a public wall in Sierre, in the Valais canton of southern Switzerland. The image started as a drawing in a pocket sketchbook and was scaled straight up to the wall, keeping the loose line of the original sketch. The title, You Are What You See, is painted as part of the piece.
