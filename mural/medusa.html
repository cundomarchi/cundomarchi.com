Medusa

🇮🇹 Parma, Italy · 2025 · 3.5m x 2.5m

Street ArtSpray Paint

Medusa with hair of blue serpents, sprayed 3.5 metres wide by 2.5 metres high in Parma, in the Emilia-Romagna region of northern Italy, in collaboration with the Italian artist Andrea. The portrait sits alongside a wall of graffiti letters, so the piece works both as a character and as part of a longer painted wall.
