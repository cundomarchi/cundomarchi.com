Flower Octopus

🇦🇷 Buenos Aires, Argentina · 2018 · 3.5m x 2.5m

Commission WorkExterior Paint & Brush

A red octopus wrapped in tropical leaves, its arms curling around a rooftop window in Buenos Aires, Argentina. The mural is 3.5 by 2.5 metres, painted with exterior paint and brush as a private commission, and it uses the existing architecture as part of the drawing: the window opening becomes the space the octopus holds on to.
