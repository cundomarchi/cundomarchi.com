Meeting of Styles

🇸🇪 Malmö, Sweden · 2025 · 5m x 3m

Street ArtMural EventSpray Paint

A surrealist face fused with layered eyes and a butterfly, sprayed across a 5 by 3 metre wall in Malmö, Sweden. It was painted live at Meeting of Styles, the international graffiti and street art festival that has been bringing writers and muralists together since the 1990s. The face is built from overlapping planes of colour rather than outlines, so the eyes read as several faces at once depending on how far back you stand.
