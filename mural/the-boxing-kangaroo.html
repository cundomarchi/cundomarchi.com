The Boxing Kangaroo

🇦🇺 Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia · 2026 · 2.5m x 2.5m

Street ArtSpray Paint

Australia's boxing kangaroo in blue shorts and red boxing gloves, standing in front of a burst of purple and green rays, sprayed 2.5 by 2.5 metres on a legal graffiti wall on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia. The boxing kangaroo is a national symbol in Australia, and the mural sits on a wall shared with other artists, next to their pieces.
