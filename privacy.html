Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What this website collects

This portfolio collects the information you choose to send through the quote form: your name, contact details, project information and any wall photograph you attach. The form is processed by FormSubmit and delivered to cundomarchi@gmail.com so Cundo Marchi can respond to your enquiry.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is used to understand visits, page views, mural views, language changes and completed quote enquiries. Personalised advertising and Google advertising signals are disabled. Before you choose, Consent Mode sends a denied analytics-storage signal; analytics cookies are created only after you accept.

Cookies and local storage

If accepted, Google Analytics may use _ga and _ga_<measurement-id> first-party cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. Google documents default retention of up to two years. This site also stores your analytics choice in your browser under cmz_analytics_consent_v1. You can change it at any time with the Cookies button.

External services

Links to Instagram, Facebook and YouTube open those services, which apply their own privacy policies. The quote form uses FormSubmit to transmit the details you submit. Please do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for a mural enquiry.

Your choices

You can reject optional analytics cookies without losing access to the website. You can also clear this site's cookies and local storage in your browser. For questions or requests concerning information sent through the quote form, email cundomarchi@gmail.com.

This page describes the website's current technical behaviour and is not legal advice.
