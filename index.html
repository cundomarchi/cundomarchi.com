Who I Am

Hello!

Hello! I'm Cundo Marchi, a visual and urban artist from Argentina.

My beginnings in muralism were in the middle of 2012, in the streets of San Fernando, Buenos Aires, Argentina. Since then, I've been traveling through more than 15 countries, in 5 different continents.

Being part of visual and urban arts events, and in different national and international muralism meetings, spreading my unique style, a fusion between surrealism and psychodraws, where I find a balance to spread a message of conscience.

What I Offer

Services

Three ways to work together: a mural on your wall, a piece painted live in front of your guests, or a hands-on workshop for your team, anywhere in the world.

Service 01

Murals

Full-scale wall art for homes, businesses and public spaces.

Home Interior

A piece designed for a specific interior, a living room, a bedroom, a restaurant or hospitality space. More intimate in scale, built around the room's own light and layout.

Commercial Space

Murals for stores, offices, restaurants and other commercial spaces, a piece built to represent a brand or set the tone for a space customers walk into.

Exterior Wall / Public Space

Murals painted outdoors in full public view, a home facade, a storefront, a festival wall, a building. Visible from the street, built to hold color through sun, rain and years outdoors.
